## 三视图 v1

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: 3D character modeling reference turnaround sheet.
Input image role: identity and visual design reference for our original cream bunny explorer named Nuonuo. Preserve its round baby face, wide dark-brown glossy eyes, tiny peach triangular nose, blush cheeks, cream-white body, drooping rabbit ears and tiny rounded paws.
Create a clean professional ORTHOGRAPHIC THREE-VIEW CHARACTER TURNAROUND on a single wide landscape sheet: front view on left, strict right-facing side profile in middle, back view on right. Three drawings of THE EXACT SAME character, identical size and height and silhouette/proportions, feet on same horizontal baseline, plenty of separation and margins. No perspective, no 3/4 angle. Neutral even studio illumination, light neutral gray background with tiny subtle baseline and no scenery. No text, labels, arrows, UI, logos or watermark.
Important modeling preparation: character is standing upright on two legs in symmetrical relaxed A-pose, arms slightly downward OUTWARD from torso (35 degrees from vertical), paws and short legs clearly separated from body and from each other. Show enough torso and limb length to allow animation while retaining adorable large head and round tummy. Feet flat and visibly separated. Closed gentle smile and open neutral eyes, no wink, no head tilt. Ears flop outward with a small clean air gap from shoulders; no intersection with arms. Include a small round cotton tail visible in side/back. DO NOT show any clothing, backpack, scarf, orb, prop, food or accessory; this is the neutral animal base mesh reference only, no human anatomy. Use smooth refined soft designer-toy/plush form with a very short subtle velvety surface; no long fur strands, no detailed surface noise. Do not add fingers; rounded mitten paws. The side view must reveal muzzle, ear thickness, body volume, rear tail and leg shape. Back view must agree exactly with front. High quality consistent 3D-style render suitable to guide mesh construction, not a claim of actual 3D asset.

输出：nuonuo-turnaround-v1.png。实际结果的侧视图面向画面左侧；用于检查角色比例与背面，不承诺严格的工程投影一致性。需要模型生成后检查耳根、腋下、脚部和尾巴；输入单图建模工具时应使用独立正面图，不将整个三联图当作单个角色图上传。
